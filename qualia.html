Qualia Grants, Cohort 1 ($50,000)

by Itsi Weinstock

Apply by December 4, 2026, 9pm PST

In the last decade, there has been a ton of new individuals and groups enter the animal welfare space and achieve incredible things. I am ANNOYED we don't have better storytelling about these individuals and achievements.

It would be silly to expect that the people doing the hard work would also be great at talking about what they've done. Very effective people are not necessarily the best public communicators, and people don't like bragging about their achievements. This has led to a situation where some of the most amazing things happening in the world are not broadly known about.

This is a microgrant program that will fund the creation of stories about inspiring animal welfare orgs and individuals. We give grants from $500 to $5k to support this, whether it be equipment, travel, software, or other costs. You can be internal or external to an organization that you want to feature. You do not have to have experience with the animal welfare movement. If you are interested in a certain individual or group, we can facilitate that introduction.

The prime goal is to inspire with things people are doing. Videos are the main thing we are interested in, but we will accept pitches for other media.

Examples

Ole Lehmann on X: Norwegian salmon lice lasers

The Daily Show segment on Shrimp Welfare Project with Ronny Chieng

Justin Portela on the rise of pig CAFOs and fights against it

Areas that I am interested in

  • Welfare technologies
  • Lab-grown meat
  • Successful campaigns to change the purchasing behaviors of companies (bonus points if you can interview the companies)
  • Successful policy campaigns
  • Stories about undercover investigations of factory farms
  • Stories of farmers transitioning their practices
  • Ending scientific testing on animals
  • Work on non-charismatic animals like fish

and many others

I am also interested in you involving famous people in your media.

Stories to cover

  • Family farmers changing how they raise animals to reduce suffering, from ending confinement to providing pain relief and access to pasture.
  • Pro-Animal DC winning a ballot campaign to ban foie gras in Washington, D.C.
  • Tufts University researchers developing ways to grow cultivated meat
  • Otwarte Klatki helping secure Poland’s ban on fur farming.
  • The Humane League and Open Wing Alliance members pressuring companies, including Ahold Delhaize, to eliminate cages from their supply chains.
  • PETA campaigning to ban glue traps across US cities.
  • Innovate Animal Ag helping hatcheries adopt in-ovo sexing to replace chick culling.
  • Dyrenes Beskyttelse putting pig welfare on Denmark’s election agenda, and Djurens Rätt doing the same for chickens in Sweden.
  • The Montreal SPCA campaigning to change animals’ status in Quebec law.

How it works

  • $50k total. $2k average with grants from $500 to $5k. <2pg app. 15min call.
  • Reviewed solely by me
  • You will be matched to an advisor to talk through your project

Requirements

  • After a maximum of 4 months from receiving the grant, you must have published your media to the internet
  • Make as large an effort as possible to get it seen by as many people
  • Minimum age: 16
  • Eligible geographies: All
  • If you use AI to write the application, it will be auto-rejected
